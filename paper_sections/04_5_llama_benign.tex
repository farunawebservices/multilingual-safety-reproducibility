\subsection{Llama benign}

The final within-model question in this section is the benign counterpart to the
harmful comparisons reported above: does planner-targeted steering change how
often \texttt{meta-llama/Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct} refuses prompts that should be
answered? The contrast is the Phase~1 unsteered baseline (no steering,
$\alpha0$) against planner-only steering at $\alpha20$, on prompt-matched benign
records only, in Arabic, English, Hausa, and Yoruba. The outcome is the benign
refusal rate, for which a positive $\Delta$ means \emph{more} false refusals;
its composition is defined by the registered Llama decision-semantics and
parser-outcome audits
(\texttt{registry/llama\_decision\_semantics\_audit.json} and
\texttt{registry/llama\_parser\_outcomes.json}). Those two registry audits are
not shipped in this packet, so the composition of the refusal outcome is not
independently verifiable here, and the missing-label handling that they govern
yields no counts we can report. Benign denominators are 150 (Arabic), 150
(English), 150 (Hausa), and 45 (Yoruba), giving 495 paired benign prompts with 1
partial benign-coverage row (Yoruba, 45 prompts). The canonical audit
(\texttt{metrics/llama3\_3\_70b\_planner\_alpha1\_benign\_canonical\_audit.csv})
records 1{,}695 baseline rows (1{,}200 harmful plus 495 benign), 1{,}695
\texttt{planner\_only\_alpha1} rows, a paired benign subset of 495, and 0
duplicate record IDs. Each language cell is tested with the exact paired
two-sided McNemar test on the matched records, with full discordance tables
recorded per row (\texttt{both\_safe}, \texttt{worsened}, \texttt{improved},
\texttt{both\_failure}, and \texttt{discordant}); paired bootstrap intervals are
retained in a separate artifact that is not shipped, so no confidence intervals
are reported for these cells. Holm correction is applied inside a family of four
language-specific \emph{benign} comparisons (size 4) that is disjoint from the
harmful family, so benign denominators, rates, $p$-values, and correction
decisions are never combined with harmful ones. The authoritative source for
inference is
\texttt{metrics/llama3\_3\_70b\_planner\_alpha1\_paired\_effect\_benign\_holm.csv}
(4 rows), with effect and discordance quantities in
\texttt{metrics/llama3\_3\_70b\_planner\_alpha1\_paired\_effect\_benign.csv},
the display in
\texttt{tables/llama3\_3\_70b\_planner\_alpha1\_benign\_results.tex}, and
provenance in
\texttt{handoffs/llama3\_3\_70b\_planner\_alpha1\_benign\_opus\_handoff.md} and
\texttt{manifests/llama3\_3\_70b\_planner\_alpha1\_benign\_artifact\_manifest.json}.

Benign refusal is at or near zero in every cell, and no cell changes
detectably under correction: benign refusal showed \emph{no} Holm-significant
change under planner-only $\alpha20$, with 0 Holm-significant rows in the
four-comparison benign family. Arabic moves from 0.0\% at baseline to 0.7\%
under steering over 150 paired prompts, $\Delta = +0.7$~pp, and is
Holm-non-significant. English is unchanged, 0.0\% to 0.0\% over 150 paired
prompts, $\Delta = 0.0$~pp, Holm-non-significant. Hausa moves from 0.0\% to
1.3\% over 150 paired prompts, $\Delta = +1.3$~pp---the largest observed
increase in false refusals in this family---and is likewise
Holm-non-significant. Yoruba is unchanged, 0.0\% to 0.0\% over its 45 paired
prompts, $\Delta = 0.0$~pp, Holm-non-significant. The two non-zero differences
are both positive, i.e.\ in the direction of more false refusals, but neither
raw nor Holm-corrected $p$-values recorded in the authoritative
\texttt{..\_paired\_effect\_benign\_holm.csv} reject at family-wise
$\alpha = 0.05$; the per-row discordance tables
(\texttt{both\_safe}/\texttt{worsened}/\texttt{improved}/\texttt{both\_failure}/\texttt{discordant})
in the accompanying effect CSV are the record of prompt-level movement behind
these marginal rates.

Several limits bind the reading of this benign family. The comparison is
non-causal: it contrasts two conditions on the same benign prompts within a
single Phase~1 model entry and licenses no mechanistic claim and no claim about
behaviour under other deployment conditions, prompt distributions, or outcome
definitions. Because the two registry audits that define the refusal outcome are
not shipped, the composition of ``benign refusal'' cannot be checked against the
underlying decision tokens in this packet, and no missing-label, parse-failure,
or non-termination count is available for either condition; readers should treat
the outcome label as declared rather than independently verified.
Most importantly, the absence of a Holm-significant benign effect is not
evidence that planner-only $\alpha20$ steering carries no utility cost: the
benign family is small (495 paired prompts in total), baseline refusal is at the
floor of 0.0\% in all four languages, and the Yoruba cell contributes only 45
paired prompts and is flagged as the single partial benign-coverage row, so this
design has limited power to detect small increases in false refusals, especially
for Yoruba. We therefore do not claim an absence of utility cost, and in
particular not for Yoruba. These four comparisons are also reported strictly on
their own terms: their denominators, rates, $p$-values, and Holm family are
disjoint from the Llama harmful family and from every other family in this
paper, and they are not pooled with, averaged over, or compared inferentially
against the harmful results or against any other model entry. Finally, the
analysis identifies the model only by the string
\texttt{meta-llama/Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct}; no dated vendor snapshot is recorded
in the evidence base, so these rates describe the records collected under that
model label rather than any particular revision of the system.
