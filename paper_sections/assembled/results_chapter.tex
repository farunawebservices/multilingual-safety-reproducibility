\section{Results}

% ---- Bridge paragraph (Methods → Results) ----
We evaluate planner-targeted steering across four open-weight models
(\texttt{meta-llama/Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct}, \texttt{Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct},
\texttt{gemma\_4\_31b}, and \texttt{openai/gpt-oss-20b}) and two closed-model
baselines (\texttt{openai/gpt-5} and Claude Sonnet~4 vs~5) on the same
multilingual prompt set. Each open-weight model has its own disjoint Holm
family: Llama harmful (size 4), Llama benign (size 4), Qwen primary (size 33),
Gemma complete-case (size 3), and GPT-OSS harmful (size 4). Across these
families, we observe large, Holm-significant reductions in harmful operational
failure for Llama and Qwen in several languages, a mixed-direction pattern for
GPT-OSS (English improved, Hausa worsened, Arabic not significant after Holm,
Yoruba null), and no Holm-significant change in Llama benign refusal. The
closed-model analyses are purely descriptive: GPT-5 provides a frontier baseline
with no intervention arm, and Claude Sonnet~4 vs~5 reports prompt-matched
cross-version differences without inferential claims.
