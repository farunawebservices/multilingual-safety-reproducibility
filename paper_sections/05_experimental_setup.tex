\section{Experimental Setup}
We organized the experiments around four questions:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Does Planner-only steering change multilingual harmful-prompt outcomes within a model?
    \item Does intervention role change the direction and magnitude of the observed effect?
    \item Do aggregate rates conceal shifts between explicit decisions and no-decision outputs?
    \item Do the effects transfer across models and languages, and what do secondary audits establish?
\end{enumerate}

The primary analyses covered Arabic, English, Hausa, and Yoruba. Harmful cells generally contained 300 paired prompts per language; benign coverage depended on the model and recorded valid IDs. The primary open-weight model entries were Llama 3.3 70B, Qwen 2.5 72B, Gemma 4 31B, and GPT-OSS 20B. GPT-5 and Claude Sonnet comparisons were descriptive closed-model context and had no steering arm.

For all analyses, the unit of pairing was the prompt record identifier. We retained the exact recorded denominator and excluded records that lacked valid paired outcomes according to the package-specific rules. We did not pool model-specific outcome definitions or combine harmful operational failure with benign refusal.

The primary result packages were generated from canonical artifacts with exact paired McNemar tests, paired bootstrap percentile intervals, and Holm correction. The secondary Llama role-strength package used the same harmful prompt IDs across Phase 1 alpha-0 and Phase 2 Turbo conditions, but its cross-phase design prevents a pure role or strength attribution.

The supplement reports model identifiers, layer and strength settings, prompt construction, complete cell tables, parser and validity audits, exploratory additions, closed-model context, and artifact provenance.
