\section*{Author Contributions}

All authors contributed to conceptualization and to the pre-registered
evaluation design, including the definition of the correction families,
outcome labels, and per-language reporting conventions used throughout.
One subset of authors led the methodology and statistical protocol
(family construction, Holm correction, denominator accounting), another
led the software, harness, and experiment execution across the
open-weight and closed-model entries, and a third led artifact auditing,
manifest verification, and reproducibility checks. All authors
participated in drafting and revising the manuscript, with senior authors
providing supervision, project administration, and funding acquisition.
The final claim wording, including the scope restrictions stated in the
Conclusion, was reviewed and approved by all authors.

\section*{Data and Code Availability}

We release the evaluation harness, prompt templates and their
translations, scoring and parsing code, statistical analysis scripts,
per-cell metric files, the tables and figures reported in the paper, and
the run manifests and inter-stage handoffs needed to reconstruct every
reported denominator. Artifacts are available at a public GitHub
repository with an archived, version-pinned Zenodo DOI; code is licensed
under MIT and released data (prompts, aggregated metrics, manifests) under
CC-BY~4.0. We do not release raw model generations or internal registry
audit logs: the former are large and may contain harmful operational
content whose redistribution is restricted under third-party model and
API terms, and the latter contain infrastructure identifiers and
contributor-level records. In place of these, we publish the derived
labels, per-cell counts, and hashes sufficient to verify that the reported
aggregates follow from the withheld files.
