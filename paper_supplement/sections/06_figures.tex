\section{Figure Inventory}
The main paper uses four compact figures generated from canonical metric files. The supplement retains detailed per-model figures: Llama Planner-only primary effects, Qwen Planner-only effects, GPT-OSS effects, Gemma complete-case effects, calibration, adversarial audit, prompt-filter comparison, closed-model descriptive figures, and the causal-patching audit.

The main figure generator is `analysis/figures/write_iclr2027_main_figures.py`. It reads the canonical metrics under `paper_artifacts/metrics/` and writes PDF/PNG files under `paper_artifacts/figures/`.
