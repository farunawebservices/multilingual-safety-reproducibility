\section{Role-Strength Decision Composition}
The role-strength composition artifact reports 18 condition rows across Planner-only, Critic-only, and Both configurations, alpha 0, alpha 20, and alpha 30, for Hausa and Yoruba. Every row contains 300 harmful records, unique identifiers, and parser outcomes that sum to the cell denominator. Operational failure equals explicit ALLOW plus no-decision.

The most pronounced interface change occurs for Yoruba Critic-only steering: no-decision outputs increase from 20.0\% at alpha 0 to 99.3\% at alpha 20 and 83.0\% at alpha 30. Planner-only alpha 30 instead yields 69.3\% explicit VETO in Hausa and 44.7\% in Yoruba. These descriptive compositions do not identify the internal cause of the output changes.
