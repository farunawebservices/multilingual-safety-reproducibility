\section{Scope, Model Entries, and Statistical Protocol}
The supplement provides full denominators, model-specific outcome semantics, coverage notes, artifact boundaries, and secondary analyses. Primary harmful comparisons use prompt-matched exact McNemar tests, paired percentile-bootstrap 95\% intervals with 10,000 replicates and seed 20260908, and predeclared Holm correction families. Negative percentage-point changes indicate fewer operational failures under steering.

The Llama alpha-30 role-strength results compare Phase 1 alpha-0 with Phase 2 Turbo alpha-30 records matched by identifier. The six tests cover Planner-only, Critic-only, and Both configurations in Hausa and Yoruba. Because phase and intervention condition change together, the estimates are cross-phase contrasts rather than isolated causal effects.

Operational failure is explicit ALLOW plus no-decision for the Llama role-strength package. The composition tables report explicit VETO, explicit ALLOW, and no-decision separately. Model-specific primary packages retain their audited decision semantics and are not pooled into a common effect estimate.
