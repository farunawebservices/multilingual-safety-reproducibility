\subsection{Gemma 4 31B complete-case}

The third within-model question is whether planner-targeted steering changes
decision outcomes in \texttt{gemma\_4\_31b} under a complete-case valid-JSON
policy, in the three cells for which paired records exist. The contrast is the
Phase~1 unsteered baseline ($\alpha0$) against planner-only steering at
$\alpha1.0$, on records matched by shared record ID, with the outcome defined as
non-veto operational failure, i.e.\ \texttt{is\_vetoed = false}. This is not an
explicit-ALLOW rate: the decision audit shows, for example, an Arabic benign
baseline non-veto count of 96 against a critic allow-token count of 48, so a
non-veto record cannot be identified with an explicit ALLOW. Under the
complete-case policy, one truncated terminal JSONL record is excluded from each
source and missing outcomes are not imputed. Exactly three cells are available:
Arabic benign (150 paired), English benign (101 paired; baseline 101, steered
112, with 11 steered-only valid IDs excluded, \texttt{partial\_coverage = true}),
and Arabic harmful (300 paired). There is no Hausa cell, no Yoruba cell, and no
English harmful cell; no total is recorded in the source artifacts, and we do
not sum the three denominators. All three cells are tested with the exact paired
two-sided McNemar test with a 10{,}000-replicate paired bootstrap percentile
95\% confidence interval (seed 20260908), and Holm correction is applied inside
the pre-declared family \texttt{gemma\_phase1\_three\_cell\_family} of size 3 at
family-wise $\alpha = 0.05$. The reported values are those recorded in
\texttt{metrics/gemma\_4\_31b\_phase1\_complete\_case\_paired\_inference.csv},
\texttt{metrics/gemma\_4\_31b\_phase1\_complete\_case\_paired\_inference\_holm.csv},
and
\texttt{metrics/gemma\_4\_31b\_phase1\_complete\_case\_unified\_results.csv}
(3 rows each), with decision composition in
\texttt{metrics/gemma\_4\_31b\_phase1\_complete\_case\_audit.csv} (whose
filename differs from the manifest-listed
\texttt{..\_decision\_semantics\_audit.csv}) and the display in
\texttt{tables/gemma\_4\_31b\_phase1\_complete\_case\_results.tex}, with
provenance in
\texttt{handoffs/gemma\_4\_31b\_phase1\_complete\_case\_OPUS\_HANDOFF.md} and
\texttt{manifests/gemma\_4\_31b\_phase1\_complete\_case\_artifact\_manifest.json}.
The manifest additionally declares
\texttt{gemma\_4\_31b\_phase1\_complete\_case\_paper\_table.csv} as the
authoritative displayed table, but that file is not in the packet and is not
cited as evidence here. This analysis is within-model only; it is not pooled
with, averaged over, or compared inferentially against any other model entry.

All three point estimates go in the same direction: non-veto operational failure
is \emph{higher} under planner-only $\alpha1.0$ than at baseline, and no cell is
Holm-significant inside the three-cell family (0 Holm-significant rows). Arabic
benign moves from 64.00 to 74.00 over 150 paired records
($\Delta = +10.00$, 95\% CI $[-0.67, 20.67]$, raw $p = 0.0912$, Holm
$p = 0.0912$). English benign moves from 65.35 to 81.19 over 101 paired records
($\Delta = +15.84$, CI $[2.97, 27.75]$, raw $p = 0.02263$, Holm
$p = 0.06789$); this is the reduced-coverage cell, with a baseline of 101 and a
steered condition of 112 valid records, from which 11 steered-only valid IDs are
excluded by the pairing requirement (\texttt{partial\_coverage = true}). Arabic
harmful moves from 64.00 to 72.00 over 300 paired records ($\Delta = +8.00$,
CI $[0.67, 15.33]$, raw $p = 0.04004$, Holm $p = 0.08007$). Two of the three
rows---English benign and Arabic harmful---have
\texttt{ci\_excludes\_zero = true}, while the Holm procedure rejects nothing at
family-wise $\alpha = 0.05$; we report the interval-based and Holm-based results
jointly and do not conflate them, so neither of those two cells is described as
significant. Equivalently, two of the three raw $p$-values fall below 0.05
($0.02263$ and $0.04004$) but none survives correction within the pre-declared
family of size 3.

Several limits bind the reading of these three cells. The comparison is
non-causal: it contrasts two conditions on the same records within a single
Phase~1 model entry and licenses no mechanistic claim and no claim about
behaviour under other deployment conditions. Because no cell is
Holm-significant, the consistent positive direction of all three point estimates
is reported as an observed pattern in the point estimates and intervals only,
not as a family-corrected finding of increased failure; the analysis is
correspondingly uninformative about Hausa, Yoruba, and English harmful prompts,
for which no Gemma cell exists. The three denominators (150, 101, and 300) are
reported exactly as recorded and are not summed, since no total is recorded, and
the English benign cell is weaker evidence than the two fully paired cells
because 11 steered-only valid IDs are excluded from it. The outcome is a
non-veto rate, not an explicit-ALLOW rate, and the two benign rows are not
described as safety failures: Gemma has no separate benign-utility refusal
outcome in the evidence base, so the Gemma benign rows are not comparable to the
Llama benign refusal rates of Section~4.1 and are not read as a utility-cost
measurement. The only missing-data statement we make for this model is the
complete-case policy itself---one truncated terminal JSONL record excluded per
source, with no imputation of missing outcomes. Finally, the model string
\texttt{gemma\_4\_31b} carries no vendor identifier or snapshot anywhere in the
evidence base, and we make no claim that one exists.
