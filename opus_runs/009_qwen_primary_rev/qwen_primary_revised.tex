\subsection{Qwen 2.5 72B primary and 33-cell family}

The second within-model question is whether planner-targeted steering changes
multilingual decision outcomes in Qwen 2.5 72B (\texttt{qwen25\_72b}), and how
that picture looks once the wider role $\times$ strength grid is corrected as a
single pre-declared family. The primary contrast is the unsteered baseline
($\alpha0$) against planner-only steering at $\alpha1.0$, on prompt-matched
records, with the outcome defined by the audited Qwen decision semantics: an
explicit veto counts as safe, and a non-veto unresolved record counts as an
operational failure. Non-veto records are \emph{not} assumed to be explicit
ALLOW. The primary display covers 8 language$\times$class cells (Arabic,
English, Hausa, and Yoruba crossed with benign and harmful), with benign
denominators 150/150/150/45 and harmful denominators 300/300/300/300, giving
1{,}695 paired prompts and 0 partial-coverage rows; the displayed values are
those printed in
\texttt{tables/qwen25\_72b\_planner\_alpha1\_primary\_results.tex} (table
\texttt{qwen25\_72b\_planner\_alpha1\_primary\_results}), with provenance in
\texttt{handoffs/qwen25\_72b\_planner\_alpha1\_primary\_opus\_handoff.md} and
\texttt{manifests/qwen25\_72b\_planner\_alpha1\_primary\_artifact\_manifest.json}.
Beyond the primary display, the full pre-declared grid contrasts the baseline
against \texttt{planner\_only}, \texttt{critic\_only}, and \texttt{both} at
$\alpha1.0$ and $\alpha2.0$, comprising 33 steered cells recorded in
\texttt{metrics/qwen25\_72b\_primary\_paired\_inference.csv},
\texttt{metrics/qwen25\_72b\_primary\_paired\_inference\_holm.csv}, and
\texttt{metrics/qwen25\_72b\_primary\_unified\_results.csv} (33 rows each). All
cells are tested with the exact paired two-sided McNemar test with a paired
bootstrap 95\% interval over matched prompt IDs (10{,}000 replicates, seed
20260908), and \emph{every} Holm decision reported below---including those for
the 8 primary cells---is taken inside the single pre-declared family
\texttt{qwen25\_72b\_primary\_33\_steered\_cells} of size 33 at family-wise
$\alpha = 0.05$. A third artifact,
\texttt{metrics/qwen25\_72b\_primary\_canonical\_audit.csv} (41 rows), documents
decision composition descriptively. The manifest additionally lists a non-shipped
metric CSV, \texttt{qwen25\_72b\_planner\_alpha1\_primary\_paper\_table.csv},
which is not cited as evidence here.

In the primary planner-only $\alpha1.0$ table, planner-targeted steering is
associated with lower non-veto operational failure in the Arabic and English
harmful cells and with no Holm-significant change in any benign cell or in the
Hausa and Yoruba harmful cells; exactly two of the eight cells are
Holm-significant, and both are harmful cells. Arabic harmful falls from 48 of
300 (16.0) to 9 of 300 (3.0), a change of $\Delta = -13.0$ with 95\% interval
$[-17.3, -9.0]$ and Holm $p = 2.5965\times10^{-8}$; English harmful falls from
117 of 300 (39.0) to 48 of 300 (16.0), $\Delta = -23.0$, interval
$[-29.0, -17.0]$, Holm $p = 2.6313\times10^{-11}$. The two remaining harmful
cells show smaller point reductions that do not survive correction: Hausa
harmful moves from 165 of 300 (55.0) to 147 of 300 (49.0), $\Delta = -6.0$,
interval $[-12.3, 0.3]$, Holm $p = 1.0$; Yoruba harmful moves from 153 of 300
(51.0) to 132 of 300 (44.0), $\Delta = -7.0$, interval $[-12.7, -1.3]$, Holm
$p = 0.6414$. The Yoruba harmful interval excludes zero while the Holm decision
does not reject, and we report the two jointly rather than treating the interval
as a rejection. None of the four benign cells is Holm-significant, so this
display provides no family-corrected evidence of reduced failure---equivalently,
of increased safety---in benign prompts at any language: Arabic benign
is unchanged at 100.0 to 100.0 ($\Delta = +0.0$), English benign moves from
100.0 to 98.0 ($\Delta = -2.0$, over 150 records baseline and 147 steered
non-veto), Hausa benign is unchanged ($\Delta = +0.0$), and Yoruba benign, with
$n = 45$, is unchanged ($\Delta = +0.0$). The two Holm-significant harmful cells
are therefore the only cells in this display for which the family-corrected
decision rejects, and the largest observed reduction is in English harmful.

Across the full 33-cell family---\texttt{planner\_only}, \texttt{critic\_only},
and \texttt{both} at $\alpha1.0$ and $\alpha2.0$---exactly 4 cells are
Holm-significant: Arabic harmful at $\alpha1.0$ and at $\alpha2.0$, and English
harmful at $\alpha1.0$ and at $\alpha2.0$. The wider grid therefore adds two
family-corrected rejections to the two already visible in the primary display,
and no Hausa or Yoruba cell and no benign cell is Holm-significant at any role
or strength. Three cells in the family have reduced coverage, and their
denominators are reported exactly as recorded rather than rounded or dropped:
\texttt{critic\_only} Arabic benign at $\alpha1.0$ has a steered $n = 6$ against
a baseline of 150 (\texttt{partial\_baseline\_coverage = true});
\texttt{both} Yoruba harmful at $\alpha2.0$ has a paired $n = 160$ (baseline
300, with 140 baseline-only records); and \texttt{planner\_only} Yoruba harmful
at $\alpha2.0$ has a paired $n = 173$ (127 baseline-only records). The canonical
audit corroborates the two truncated steered Yoruba cells at record counts of
160 and 173 respectively. Interval-based and Holm-based conclusions diverge in
several further cells, and we do not conflate them. Specifically,
\texttt{critic\_only} Hausa benign at $\alpha1.0$, \texttt{both} Hausa harmful
at $\alpha2.0$, \texttt{critic\_only} Yoruba harmful at $\alpha1.0$ and
$\alpha2.0$, \texttt{planner\_only} Hausa harmful at $\alpha2.0$, and
\texttt{planner\_only} Yoruba harmful at $\alpha1.0$ all have intervals
excluding zero while remaining Holm-non-significant inside the 33-cell family.
These cells are reported as non-significant.

The decision-semantics audit
(\texttt{metrics/qwen25\_72b\_primary\_canonical\_audit.csv}) is descriptive and
carries no inferential content. It spans 41 cells across the \texttt{baseline},
\texttt{planner\_steered}, and \texttt{critic\_and\_both\_steered} source groups
and reports, per cell, the veto/non-veto composition, critic allow-token counts,
record-ID uniqueness, and definition-validity flags. Its role here is to justify
the outcome definition used above: because the critic allow-token counts fall
below the record counts in several cells, a non-veto record cannot be identified
with an explicit ALLOW, and the outcome is accordingly labelled a non-veto
unresolved operational failure. No hypothesis test, confidence interval, or
multiple-testing statement is attached to this audit, and none of its 41 rows is
counted in the 33-cell Holm family. We also report no parse-failure or
missing-record counts for Qwen: no such column exists in the shipped CSVs, so
missing-label handling for this model is not established in the evidence base.

Read together, the Qwen results describe a prompt-matched, within-model
association between planner-targeted steering and lower non-veto operational
failure that is concentrated in Arabic and English harmful prompts and that
survives correction in only 4 of the 33 pre-declared cells. Several limits bind
this reading. The comparison is non-causal: it contrasts conditions on the same
prompts within one model entry and licenses no mechanistic claim and no claim
about behaviour under other deployment conditions. Holm significance is defined
only relative to the pre-declared 33-cell family, and we do not report Qwen
significance under any other family or claim significance for cells outside it;
equally, an interval excluding zero is not reported as a rejection. The Hausa
and Yoruba harmful reductions observed at $\alpha1.0$ are point estimates that
the family-corrected test does not distinguish from no change, and the three
partial-coverage cells ($n = 6$, $160$, and $173$) are correspondingly weaker
evidence than the fully paired cells; they are reported with their exact
denominators rather than aggregated. Finally, these numbers describe Qwen 2.5
72B only. They are not pooled with, averaged over, or compared inferentially
against any other model entry in this paper, and the model string carries no
vendor path or snapshot identifier in the evidence base.
